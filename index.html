BEFORE YOU START

HTSUS in the U.S., NCM in Brazil. The first 6 digits are the common base.

The common language is the Harmonized System. After digit 6, each market can open the code in a different way.

🇺🇸 United States

HTSUS

8708305090

Ex: 8708.30 is already an HS6 code. The last 4 digits add U.S.-specific detail.

🇧🇷 Brazil

NCM

87083090

Ex: Brazil keeps the same HS6 base and adds 2 Mercosur digits.

PRACTICAL TIP

Comparing countries? Start at HS6.

Ex: To compare a U.S. HTSUS10 with a Brazilian NCM8, first go back to the common 6-digit code. After that, use a concordance if you need more detail.

Language note: Portuguese HS2, HS4 and HS6 labels follow official Brazilian nomenclature. HTS8 and HTSUS10 are U.S.-specific; whenever a Portuguese adaptation is used at those levels, it is an AI-assisted free translation for readability, not official nomenclature. The USITC English text remains the reference.

WHAT DO THESE NUMBERS MEAN?

Four measures. Four different uses.

The important part is knowing which one fits the question you are trying to answer.

01

General imports

Everything that physically arrived in the U.S.

Ex: A shipment arrives and goes to a bonded warehouse. It already counts here.
Use for: the broadest view of import flows entering the country.
02

Imports for consumption

What was cleared to enter U.S. consumption channels.

Ex: That same shipment leaves the bonded warehouse two months later. It counts here at that moment.
Use for: questions closer to market entry, customs and tariffs.
03

Dutiable value

The part of consumption imports that was subject to duty.

Ex: US$100m entered for consumption and US$40m was dutiable. Then 40% of the flow was exposed to duty.
Use for: seeing how much of the imported value was actually dutiable.
04

Calculated duties

The Census estimate of duties calculated on the imports.

Ex: US$2m in calculated duties over US$40m dutiable value gives an observed effective rate of 5%.
Careful: this is not the legal tariff rate and may not equal what was actually paid.
